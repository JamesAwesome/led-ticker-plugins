The other states: goal, final, next game

Same palette as the live board. The goal section is animated and loops every 4 seconds. Intermission isn't drawn: it reuses the live board with the clock replaced by 2ND INT 14:12.

1 · Goal celebration (plays on the widget's next turn)

Three quick flashes in the scoring team's color with GOAL knocked out. Then the new score, where the scoring team's number pulses, plus the scorer with their season goal count and the assists.





2 · Final (overtime)

Same board as live. The winner's score turns green, the loser's dims, and the status reads FINAL / OT (or SO). Stays up for final_hold_hours, like baseball.





3 · Next game (teams mode, no game today)

Matchup in team colors, day and time in amber, arena dimmed.
