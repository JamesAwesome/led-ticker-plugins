LED-TICKER · WEATHER PLUGIN

Weather — Multi-Day Forecast

A new forecast mode in the dot-matrix language of the flight tracker. Conditions are hand-built LED glyphs; hi​/​lo temps run warm-orange over cool-blue. The hi-res signs get a today hero + forecast strip and scale the day count to their width — smallsign 3-day, bigsign 5-day, longboi 7-day.
